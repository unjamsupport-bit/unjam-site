Privacy Policy for Unjam: Arrow Puzzle Escape

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Unjam: Arrow Puzzle Escape ("Unjam", "the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. Unjam is developed and published independently. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at unjam.support@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect

Unjam is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require an account, a login, or any personal profile to play. We do not collect your name, email address, or contact list.

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect limited technical data as described below:

Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics)

Collects app usage data such as which screens and features you use, session length, and gameplay events (for example, level started, level completed, hint used, purchase completed). This data is associated with an app-instance identifier generated on your device, not with your name or email. We use it to understand which levels are too hard or too easy and to improve the game.

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) (advertising)

Shows the ads that keep Unjam free to play (between levels, and as optional "watch an ad" rewards for hints or continuing a level). To do this, the SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking — see Section 3), IP address (used to estimate general region and for fraud prevention), device information, and data about the ads shown to you and how you interact with them. Some of this data may be used by Google and its advertising partners to show you more relevant ads and to measure ad performance across apps, which is why it falls under Apple's "used to track you" category.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Manages the consent choices described in Section 3 (for example, the GDPR consent form in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, or applicable US state privacy choices). It stores your consent choice on your device so we and our advertising partners know which type of ads to show you.

Apple StoreKit

Handles in-app purchases (Remove Ads, hint packs) directly with your Apple ID. We do not see or store your payment details; Apple processes the transaction, and our app and its analytics only record which product you purchased so the purchase can be applied and restored.

We do not use any other analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service beyond the ones listed above. We do not access your contacts, photos, location via GPS, microphone, or camera — the App does not request any of those permissions.

2. How we use data

We use the data described above to:

  • keep the App working and your purchases and progress consistent;
  • understand how the game is played so we can fix bugs and balance level difficulty;
  • show ads that fund free access to the App, and measure how those ads perform;
  • meet legal and App Store requirements around consent and tracking.

We do not sell your data. We do not combine analytics data with any information that identifies you personally, such as your name or email — because we never collect that information in the first place.

3. Your choices: App Tracking Transparency and consent

On iOS, the first time an advertising identifier would be used, the App shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or deny tracking at any time:

  • If you deny tracking, the App does not access your device's advertising identifier, and you are shown non-personalized ads instead of personalized ones.
  • You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

Where required (for example, in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, and under applicable US state privacy laws), a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads are requested, letting you choose what kind of advertising and measurement you allow.

4. Data retention

Analytics and advertising data are retained by Google according to their own retention policies (see Section 6). Your local game progress, hints, and settings are stored on your device and, where applicable, in your iCloud/Game Center account if you use device backup — we do not keep a separate copy on our own servers, because Unjam does not use one.

5. Children's privacy

Unjam is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not ask for age, name, or any other personal identifier. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at unjam.support@gmail.com and we will address it.

6. Third-party privacy policies

For details on how our service providers handle data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase data collection: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps using its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export data associated with your device, or to object to certain processing (for example, under GDPR in the EEA/UK, or CCPA/CPRA in California). Because Unjam does not collect personally identifying information directly, most such requests should be directed to Google as described in Section 6, using the identifiers/controls in Apple's Tracking settings (Section 3). For anything related to purchases or app behavior, contact us at unjam.support@gmail.com and we will help however we can.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes (for example, if we add a new analytics or advertising partner). We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, note it in the App's "What's New" release notes.
